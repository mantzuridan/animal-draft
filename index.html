📖 How to play

1. The draft. You and your opponent each have 20 coins. Animals come up one at a time and you bid for them. Whoever opens must bid at least 1. Raise by 1 or choose your own amount. Whoever passes loses the animal; the highest bidder pays.

The sports. There are 8 sports, and every game uses 5 random ones. You see them at the top of the screen before the first animal comes up.

2. Secret skills. Every animal has a hidden score in each sport. Use what you know about animals!

3. Out of coins? You lose your turns. Your opponent may buy each new animal for 1 coin, or it goes to you for free. When one team has 5 animals, the other team gets random ones.

4. Pick your athletes. Put one animal in each of the 5 sports. You have 90 seconds, and your opponent picks in secret.

5. The games. Each sport is your animal vs your opponent's. Higher score wins; a tie is a coin flip. Win 3 sports to win the game!

Opponents. Play a friend online with a room code, or the bot. The bot manages its budget, values animals by what its team still needs, and picks its athletes carefully.

🗂️ Album. Every animal starts as a shadow. Win a game with it in your team to unlock it. Tap an animal to see its record in each sport.

🏆 Achievements. Each one has 4 medals: 🥉 🥈 🥇 💎, earned in order.

⭐ Stars. Each sport you win gives 1 ⭐, winning the game gives 5 more, and a 5–0 gives another 5. Medals give 5 / 10 / 25 / 75. Spend stars on skins in the 🛒 Shop, and choose what each animal wears in 🎨 Skins.

Don't wait too long: after 20 seconds the game bids +1 for you.
