📖 How to play

1. The draft. You and the bot each have 20 coins. Animals come up one at a time and you bid for them. Whoever opens must bid at least 1. Raise by 1 or choose your own amount. Whoever passes loses the animal; the highest bidder pays.

2. Secret skills. Every animal has a hidden score in each sport. Use what you know about animals!

3. Out of coins? You lose your turns. The bot may buy each new animal for 1 coin, or it goes to you for free. When one team has 5 animals, the other team gets random ones.

4. Pick your athletes. Put one animal in each of the 5 sports. You have 90 seconds, and the bot picks in secret.

5. The games. Each sport is your animal vs the bot's. Higher score wins; a tie is a coin flip. Win 3 sports to win the game!

Bot level. 🐣 Easy is a simple bot. 🔥 Hard manages its budget, values animals by what its team still needs, and picks its athletes carefully. Stats are kept separately for each level.

Don't wait too long: after 20 seconds the game bids +1 for you.
