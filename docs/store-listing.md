## Full description
Who wins the high jump: a puma or a kangaroo? A shark or a dolphin in the pool?

Animal Draft is a quick strategy game of bidding and guessing. Each game draws 5 sports from 8: sprint, swimming, weightlifting, high jump, javelin, marathon, climbing and archery. Animals come up one at a time, and you and your opponent bid for them with 20 coins. Spend too much on one star and your team runs short. Wait too long and the best animals are gone.

Once both teams have 5 animals, you secretly pick which animal goes to which sport. Win 3 sports and you win the game.

- 40 animals, each with its own strengths. Nobody shows you the numbers, so you learn them by playing.
- Play the bot, or a friend online with a 4-letter room code.
- Fill your animal album and earn medals for achievements.
- Earn stars and dress your animals in skin packs: Babies, Pixel, Clay, Origami, Business, Pirates and the Gold trophy skin.
